The most accurate model was looking at the wrong thing

PneumoScan · project summary · live demo pneumoscan-nine.vercel.app · code github.com/Prathamesh250205/PneumoScan-CNN

Three ImageNet-pretrained CNNs were fine-tuned to detect pneumonia in pediatric chest X-rays (Kermany et al., 5,856 images). ResNet18 scored highest, but only 44% of its Grad-CAM attention fell on the lungs, barely above the 39% expected by chance. MobileNetV2 was 2.2 points less accurate and put 71% there. On 656 adult X-rays from another hospital, every model lost about 0.2 AUC and ResNet18's lead disappeared.

2 · Where the models look

Lungs, clavicles and scapulae were segmented with torchxrayvision's ChestX-Det PSPNet. For each image we measured the share of Grad-CAM energy (last conv block, predicted class) inside the lung mask. Normal X-rays are the clean comparison, because the adult-trained segmenter drops some consolidated lung on pediatric pneumonia X-rays.

A correction to this project's first, visual claim: ResNet18's attention on the clavicles and scapulae (4.7%) is below their 6.4% share of the image. Its attention isn't on the shoulders. It's spread over the neck, mediastinum and borders, and mostly not on the lungs.

  • ResNet18's pediatric lead is significant (paired bootstrap ΔAUC −0.011, CI −0.019 to −0.003 for MobileNetV2 − ResNet18) but vanishes externally. MobileNetV2 ranks first, though +0.015 (CI −0.020 to +0.051) is not significant.
  • Thresholds don't transfer. At the pediatric 0.946, ResNet18 flags 80% of healthy adults (specificity 0.199).
  • Lung-focused attention did not buy measurable robustness under this shift, which mixes a new hospital with a new age group.

Method and system

  • Training: PyTorch, ImageNet weights, 2-class head, Adam 1e-4, 10 epochs, flips and ±10° rotation (Kaggle T4).
  • Preprocessing: grayscale → 3 channels → 224² bilinear → ImageNet normalisation. Identical in training and serving.
  • Statistics: stratified bootstrap (2,000 draws), paired across models on the same images.
  • Serving: ONNX Runtime + FastAPI as a Vercel function. The ONNX graphs also output the class activation map, which equals Grad-CAM for global-average-pool heads (verified correlation 1.0), so live heatmaps need no backward pass.
  • Testing: Playwright end-to-end test on desktop and mobile, run against local and production.

Limitations

  • One training run per model, so 1–3 point gaps between models may be partly seed noise.
  • The external test mixes a hospital shift with an age shift (children → adults).
  • Lung masks come from a segmentation model, not radiologists.
  • The validation split has 16 images, so operating points were cross-validated on the test set and are optimistic for new sites.
Research demonstration only. Not a medical device. Not validated for clinical use and not cleared by any regulator. Do not use for diagnostic decisions.

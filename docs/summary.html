The most accurate model was looking at the wrong thing

PneumoScan · project summary · live demo pneumoscan-nine.vercel.app · code github.com/Prathamesh250205/PneumoScan-CNN

Three ImageNet-pretrained CNNs were fine-tuned to detect pneumonia in pediatric chest X-rays (Kermany et al.), each with three training seeds. ResNet18 was the most accurate (88%), yet only 47% of its Grad-CAM attention fell on the lungs, barely above the 39% expected by chance. MobileNetV2 put 67% there, in every seed. All three rank cases equally well (AUC 0.954–0.960). On 656 adult X-rays from another hospital every model degraded, and MobileNetV2 degraded least.

Kermany test split, n = 624 (234 normal, 390 pneumonia), threshold 0.5. The original single run gave ResNet18 0.978 AUC; retraining gives 0.940–0.966, so that lead was a lucky draw. For the served ResNet18, a threshold chosen for 95% sensitivity by cross-validation (t = 0.946, used by the live demo) raises specificity from 0.68 to 0.88.

2 · Where the models look

Lungs, clavicles and scapulae were segmented with torchxrayvision's ChestX-Det PSPNet. For each image we measured the share of Grad-CAM energy (last conv block, predicted class) inside the lung mask. Normal X-rays are the clean comparison, because the adult-trained segmenter drops some consolidated lung on pediatric pneumonia X-rays.

Correction to this project's first, visual claim: the served ResNet18's attention on clavicles and scapulae (4.7%) is below their 6.4% share of the image. It isn't on the shoulders; it's just not on the lungs.

  • The lung-focused model degrades least on average. Seed by seed, MobileNetV2 − ResNet18 NIH AUC is −0.017, +0.078 and +0.026: a trend, not a significant result, with three seeds.
  • Thresholds don't transfer. At the pediatric 0.946, the served ResNet18 flags 80% of healthy adults (specificity 0.199).
  • The shift mixes a new hospital with a new age group, and it hurts every model.

Method and system

  • Training: PyTorch, ImageNet weights, 2-class head, Adam 1e-4, 10 epochs, flips and ±10° rotation; 3 seeds per model (Kaggle T4).
  • Preprocessing: grayscale → 3 channels → 224² bilinear → ImageNet normalisation. Identical in training and serving.
  • Statistics: stratified bootstrap (2,000 draws), paired across models on the same images.
  • Serving: ONNX Runtime + FastAPI as a Vercel function. The ONNX graphs also output the class activation map, which equals Grad-CAM for global-average-pool heads (verified correlation 1.0), so live heatmaps need no backward pass.
  • Testing: Playwright end-to-end test on desktop and mobile, run against local and production.

Limitations

  • Three seeds per model: enough to show stability, too few for formal tests between architectures.
  • The external test mixes a hospital shift with an age shift (children → adults).
  • Lung masks come from a segmentation model, not radiologists.
  • The validation split has 16 images, so operating points were cross-validated on the test set and are optimistic for new sites.
Research demonstration only. Not a medical device. Not validated for clinical use and not cleared by any regulator. Do not use for diagnostic decisions.

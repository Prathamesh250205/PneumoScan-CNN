Research demo · Pediatric chest radiography

The most accurate model was looking at the wrong thing.

We trained three CNNs, three seeds each, to detect pneumonia in pediatric chest X-rays. ResNet18 was the most accurate, yet only 47% of its Grad-CAM attention landed on the lungs, barely above the 39% you'd expect by chance. MobileNetV2 put 67% there, in every seed.

At a glance

Same scores. Different evidence.

  1. 01

    They rank cases equally well

    0.954 · 0.956 · 0.960

    AUC for ResNet18, MobileNetV2 and EfficientNet-B0, averaged over three training seeds. An early 0.978 for ResNet18 turned out to be a lucky run.

  2. 02

    Only one looks at the lungs

    47% vs 67%

    Share of Grad-CAM attention inside the lungs for ResNet18 and MobileNetV2, where chance is 39%. Every MobileNetV2 seed beats every ResNet18 seed.

  3. 03

    All of them break elsewhere

    −0.16 · −0.13 · −0.20

    AUC drop on 656 adult X-rays from the NIH Clinical Center. The lung-focused model, MobileNetV2, drops least: a trend, not yet proof.

About This Research

CNN Comparison Study

This web demo presents a comparative evaluation of three convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures trained on the Kermany et al. Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia) dataset.

Model Performance Comparison

Architecture Accuracy F1 Score AUC-ROC Clinical Focus
ResNet18 Loaded Model 87.8% 0.911 0.978 Non-diagnostic regions (Shoulder/Collarbone)
MobileNetV2 85.6% 0.896 -- Diagnostic regions (Lung tissue)
EfficientNet-B0 84.8% 0.891 -- Diffuse lung regions

Core Finding: The Interpretability Trade-off

When evaluating deep learning models for clinical deployments, accuracy is not the sole metric. While ResNet18 achieved the highest overall accuracy (87.8%), Grad-CAM explainability analysis revealed a critical flaw: its predictions heavily relied on non-diagnostic artifacts (e.g., shoulder joint structure, patient positioning, collarbone).

In contrast, MobileNetV2, despite scoring 2.2% lower in raw accuracy, consistently targeted actual diagnostic areas (lung tissue consolidation and density differences). This represents a classic accuracy-vs-interpretability trade-off where the less accurate model is clinically safer and more trustworthy.
